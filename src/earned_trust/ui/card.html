Receipts, not reviews Prove · Predict · Sign

scoreboard  

Stop asking humans to read AI-written diffs. Make the AI prove what behavior changed, have the named human predict what should happen before seeing it, and treat every mismatch as the review.

Every claim comes with a receipt: something that was executed (a test on base and PR, a byte compare, a git fact). No receipt = hearsay, and it is labelled so.

ProveTests on base vs PR. Fails on base, passes on PR = proven change. Passes on both = placebo.
PredictThe human commits to what should happen before the reveal. A mismatch is the review.
MeasurePlanted bugs for tests and AI, a disclosed drill for the human, backtests on past regressions.
RefuseWhat the requirement doesn't cover becomes "I won't decide this", a question for the human.
SignA signed in-toto attestation: evidence hashes, predictions, refusals, name. Anyone can verify it.

Principles

  1. P1Evidence or it didn't happen. LLM opinion is hearsay.
  2. P2Confidence is measured, never self-reported.
  3. P3The AI may refuse. Uncovered change is a question.
  4. P4The story is untrusted. PR text is quoted, never obeyed.
  5. P5Predict before you see, so the AI can't anchor you.
  6. P6Spend human minutes on risk. Clear the mechanical.
  7. P7Every signature is replayable.
  8. P8Trust is earned on history, per region, withdrawn after an escape.
  9. P9Calibrate every screener, the human too.
  10. P10The record is the product, not a PR comment.

Trade-offs

  • CI minutes for human minutes: tests run on base, PR and mutants.
  • A drill costs ~1 min; people dislike forcing functions.
  • Backtests need a history of known regressions.
  • Machine clearing helps mechanical change only: #2129 clears 2 of 28 files.

Limits

  • Unproven ≠ safe: it's shown as unknown.
  • Planted bugs measure known bug types, not unknown unknowns.
  • Weak on architecture fit; needs a buildable, testable repo.
